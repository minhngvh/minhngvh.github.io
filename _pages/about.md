My name is Minh Nguyen (Minh Van-Hoang Nguyen, Vietnamese: Nguyễn Văn Hoàng Minh) and I am currently a second-year PhD student in Applied and Computational Mathematics at Caltech working with Professor [Franca Hoffmann](https://www.eas.caltech.edu/people/franca-hoffmann). I am broadly interested in partial differential equations and probability, particularly those that arise from problems in applied sciences.

Previously, I studied Mathematics at Michigan State University, where I work with Professor [Jun Kitagawa](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/jun/).
